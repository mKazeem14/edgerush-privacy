Privacy Policy for EdgeRush

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game EdgeRush handles information. The game is developed by Kazeem Abbas. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or any other personal details. We do not run our own servers that store information about you.

Your game progress (unlocked levels, coins, purchases made with in-game coins, sound setting) is stored only on your device. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and use certain information from your device, such as:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable identifier on your device)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and operating system information (model, OS version, language, screen size)
  • App usage and ad interaction information (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped)

This information is collected and processed by Google under Google's own privacy policy. You can read more here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, the exact path can vary by device).

3. Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region that requires it, the app shows a consent form (using Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested. You can choose whether to allow personalized advertising. If consent is not given, ads may not be shown or may be non-personalized.

4. How we use information

We do not collect personal information ourselves, so we do not use, sell or share it. The only data processed outside your device is the advertising-related data handled by Google, as described above.

5. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because game data is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app. For data held by Google, please use Google's account and ads controls.

7. Security

We do not transmit your game data to our own servers. We rely on the security of your device and of Google's services for advertising.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this address, with the "Last updated" date changed accordingly.
