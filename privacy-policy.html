Privacy Policy for EdgeRush

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game EdgeRush: Pixel Parkour (the "app") handles information. The app is developed by Kazeem Abbas ("we", "us"). By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location or any other personal details. We do not run our own servers that store information about you.

Your game progress (unlocked levels, coins, purchases made with in-game coins, sound setting) is stored only on your device. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob and mediation partners)

The app shows ads using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. Through AdMob mediation, the app may also show ads from third-party ad networks, currently including Unity Ads, provided by Unity Technologies. We may add or remove ad partners over time.

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the SDKs of our ad partners may automatically collect and use certain information from your device, such as:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable identifier on your device)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and operating system information (model, OS version, language, screen size, network type)
  • App usage and ad interaction information (for example, whether an ad was shown, completed or tapped)

This information is collected and processed by Google and our ad partners under their own privacy policies. You can read more here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Unity Privacy Policy

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads; the exact path can vary by device).

3. Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region that requires it, the app shows a consent form (using Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested. You can choose whether to allow personalized advertising, and you can change your choice later where the app provides that option. If consent is not given, ads may not be shown or may be non-personalized.

4. How we use information

We do not collect personal information ourselves, so we do not use, sell or share it. The only data processed outside your device is the advertising-related data handled by Google and our ad partners, as described above.

5. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because game data is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app. For data held by Google or our ad partners, please use their account and ads controls or contact them directly through the privacy policies linked above.

7. Security

We do not transmit your game data to our own servers. We rely on the security of your device and of the services provided by Google and our ad partners for advertising.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we add or change ad partners. The latest version will always be available at this address, with the "Last updated" date changed accordingly.
